## Evidence and GEO limits

Three focused US-English SERP snapshots covered `html to design alternative`,
`html to figma`, and `claude design to figma`. AI Overview features were
reported, but the responses did not include their text or citation URLs.
Do not infer our presence, absence, or causal click loss from that flag.

Google states that standard SEO applies to AI features: accessible,
indexable, helpful textual content and consistent structured data, not a
special AI schema or mandatory AI text file. Citation gains remain unmeasured.
