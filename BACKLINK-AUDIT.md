# html2design Backlink Audit

Audit date: 2026-07-29
Source: DataForSEO live backlink summary

## Measured baseline

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Backlinks | 5 |
| Referring domains | 4 |
| DataForSEO domain rank | 0 |
| DataForSEO spam score | 36 |

The profile is too small to support authority-led growth. Two discovered links were low-quality or spam-like domains, while the useful mentions included a safety/reputation profile and one relevant Vietnamese article. This is not evidence of a search penalty, but it means rankings currently depend mostly on page relevance and the Figma Community listing rather than independent authority.

## Risks

- The domain has almost no independent corroboration.
- Low-quality links form too large a share of a five-link profile.
- A few new spam links can distort percentage-based toxicity metrics at this size.
- Buying bulk links would increase risk without improving product credibility.

## Acquisition priorities

1. Keep the Figma Community listing linked to the canonical homepage and the verified result-quality page.
2. Publish reproducible conversion examples with source URL, viewport, mode, date, screenshots, and known differences.
3. Earn tutorial mentions from Figma, frontend, no-code, and AI coding communities by providing tested examples rather than generic guest posts.
4. Add accurate profiles to relevant product directories after checking for duplicates.
5. Ask partners or creators who already demonstrate the plugin to link to the exact supporting guide or result page.

## Measurement rule

Track verified referring domains, referral sessions, assisted conversions, and retained rankings. Do not use raw backlink count as the primary KPI. Re-run the live profile monthly until at least 20 independently verified referring domains exist.
