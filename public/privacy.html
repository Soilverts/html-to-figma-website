Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 5, 2026

1. Introduction

HTML to Figma ("we", "our", or "us") operates the website html2design.com and the HTML to Figma plugin for Figma (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Service.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account & Payment Information: When you purchase a subscription, payment is processed through Gumroad. We receive your email address and transaction details but do not store your credit card or payment credentials. Please refer to Gumroad's Privacy Policy for their data practices.
  • Support Communications: If you contact us via email, we collect the content of your message and your email address to respond to your inquiry.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: Our website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may collect standard web analytics data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • Plugin Data: The Figma plugin processes HTML/CSS content locally within your browser. We do not transmit, store, or have access to the HTML/CSS code you convert, nor the resulting Figma designs.

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personal data beyond what is described above.
  • We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising purposes on html2design.com.
  • We do not access, store, or transmit the content of your Figma files.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To process and manage your subscription
  • To provide customer support and respond to inquiries
  • To send transactional emails related to your purchase (via Gumroad)
  • To improve and maintain the Service
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may process your data:

Service Purpose Data Processed
Gumroad Payment processing Email, payment info
Cloudflare Website hosting & CDN IP address, web analytics
Figma Plugin platform Plugin usage (per Figma's policy)

5. Data Retention

We retain your email address and transaction records for as long as your subscription is active and for a reasonable period thereafter for accounting and legal compliance purposes. Support correspondence is retained for up to 24 months.

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at support@html2design.com.

6. Data Security & Local Processing

HTML to Figma processes all HTML/CSS content 100% locally within your browser. Your code and generated Figma designs never leave your device — nothing is uploaded to or processed on our servers. We have no ability to access, view, or store any content you convert.

The only personal data we hold is your email address and transaction record from Gumroad, which is the minimum required to manage your subscription. Our website is served over HTTPS via Cloudflare.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Portability: Request a copy of your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to processing of your data in certain circumstances.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at support@html2design.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child under 16, we will take steps to delete that information.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. These countries may have data protection laws that differ from your jurisdiction. By using the Service, you consent to the transfer of your information to these countries.
