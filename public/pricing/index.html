Pricing FAQ

Is HTML to Figma free?

html2design is a paid plugin. It costs $12/month or $96/year. You need a Figma account (any plan, including free) to install and subscribe. There's no free ongoing tier, but you only pay for html2design — not for a Figma upgrade.

What does html2design cost?

html2design costs $12 per month billed monthly, or $96 per year ($8/month) billed annually. Both plans include unlimited HTML-to-Figma conversions with full feature access. No import limits, no seat tiers, no enterprise add-ons.

What are the limits on the free plan?

There is no ongoing free plan. html2design requires a subscription ($12/month or $96/year) to convert HTML to Figma. The subscription is managed through the Figma Plugin Marketplace — no separate account or billing portal needed.

Do I need a paid Figma plan to use html2design?

No. html2design works with any Figma account — including the free Figma Starter plan. The plugin subscription is completely separate from your Figma plan. You do not need Figma Professional, Organization, or Enterprise to use html2design.

Is there a team or bulk discount?

There is no dedicated team plan. Each designer or developer subscribes individually on their own Figma account at $12/month or $96/year per person. The annual plan ($96/year) is the best option for individuals who use html2design regularly.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. html2design is billed through the Figma Plugin Marketplace. You can cancel your subscription at any time in your Figma settings under Billing. Monthly subscriptions cancel at the end of the current billing period; annual subscriptions are not prorated but can be cancelled to prevent renewal.

How does html2design compare to free alternatives?

Free alternatives typically produce screenshot-based or flat image imports — layers you cannot edit. html2design converts HTML and CSS into native Figma layers: editable text nodes, rectangles, groups, and frames that preserve your design structure.
