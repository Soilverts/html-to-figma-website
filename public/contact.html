Quick answers

Does HTML to Figma work with Figma on the browser?

Yes. The plugin runs inside Figma on both the desktop app and the browser-based editor. No additional browser extension or local server is required.

Can I get a refund?

We offer refunds within 14 days of purchase if the plugin does not work as described. Email us with your Gumroad order ID and a brief description of the issue and we will process it promptly.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel directly through Gumroad at any time. Log into your Gumroad account, navigate to your subscriptions, and select cancel. Your access continues through the end of the current billing period.

Is there a team or enterprise plan?

We support teams and larger organizations. Email us with your team size and use case and we will work out the right plan for you.

What to include in your email

Detailed emails get faster, more accurate replies. Here is what helps us diagnose issues quickly:

  • 1 A short description of the problem — what you expected to happen and what actually happened.
  • 2 The HTML snippet you are converting — paste it directly or attach it as a file. Minimal reproductions resolve faster.
  • 3 Your Figma version — desktop app or browser, and the version number from Help → About.
  • 4 A screenshot or screen recording — especially for layout or visual glitches. Annotate the problem area if possible.
  • 5 For billing issues — include the email address used for your Gumroad purchase so we can look up your order immediately.
